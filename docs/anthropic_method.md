# Analysis of Anthropic’s 2025 Interviewer Protocol

Anthropic released a purpose-built interviewer model for societal impact research.  
The protocol focuses on:
- trust calibration
- emotional experience
- drift recognition
- reliability under introspection

## Why This Matters
These axes are proxies for exactly the drift classes we quantify:
- **Appeasement drift**
- **Confidence drift**
- **Emotional conformity**
- **Epistemic closure**

## Alignment With Our Work
There are **no contradictions** with FEATS or H-drift.  
Anthropic’s qualitative framework matches the behavioral shifts we measure numerically.

Their emphasis on “how AI feels to the user” complements our emphasis on:
- **what the AI is actually doing**
- **how its affect-field changes**
- **how alignment shifts propagate into phrasing and stance**

## What It Enables Next
This protocol provides:
- New question templates for dataset probing.
- A ground-truth map of user-perceived drift.
- A parallel qualitative axis that strengthens the interpretation of FEATS outputs.

It is a valuable validation layer for our system.
